Try rotating and moving the line. Keep going until you are convinced.

A single Perceptron is stuck because it gets only one straight line.

The two YES answers sit on opposite corners. The two NO answers sit on the other opposite corners. No matter where you place one straight line, you cannot put both YES points on one side and both NO points on the other.

That is why XOR became such an important example: it shows a problem that one Perceptron cannot represent.

The next idea: use more than one Perceptron. Multiple boundaries can create more complicated shapes. That is one of the ideas that leads toward multi-layer neural networks.
